# SPIKE — Pentest vendor research (credit card payment)

**Conducted by:** 4Shark Team
**Date:** 2026-03-26
**Status:** Closed — see conclusions

---

## Goal

Find penetration testing companies that accept credit card payment for 4Shark's pentest needs.

---

## Method

- Web research for international and Brazilian pentest companies
- Filtered for companies that explicitly accept credit card as payment method
- Compared pricing, certifications, and service models

---

## Evidence

### International Companies

| Company | Pricing | Payment | Notes |
|---------|---------|---------|-------|
| **Pentest-Tools.com** | Plans on [pricing page](https://pentest-tools.com/pricing) | Credit/debit card via Stripe, wire transfer | Self-service platform, automated + manual |
| **Astra Security** | Scanner: $199/mo, Pentest: $5,999/yr, Enterprise: $9,999/yr | Credit card, $7/week trial available | Automated + manual pentest |
| **Cobalt.io** | Starting ~$65,000/yr (enterprise-focused) | Credit-based model, contact sales | OSCP-certified pentesters, PtaaS model |
| **Cyver Core** | Plans on [pricing page](https://core.cyver.io/pricing/) | Credit/debit card via Stripe, SEPA | Flexible payment forms |

### Brazilian Companies

| Company | Pricing | Payment | Notes |
|---------|---------|---------|-------|
| **Solyd Offensive Security** | Contact for quote | Credit card up to 12x (with interest), Pix, transfer | Experience with OpenBanking Brasil, Caixa, Crefisa |
| **Avant Services** | Contact for quote | Credit card up to 10x without interest | [Site](https://avantservices.com.br/pentest) |
| **Vantico** | R$15,000 — R$100,000+ | Contact for payment options | Reference in offensive security in Brazil |
| **Pentest Brasil** | Contact for quote | Contact for payment options | Certifications: OSCP, CRTO, CRTP, eWPTX |

### Pricing Reference (General Market)

- Average pentest cost: $5,000 — $50,000
- Professional pentests: typically $5,000 — $15,000, can exceed $30,000
- Brazilian market: R$15,000 — R$100,000+

---

## Conclusions

- **Best international options for credit card**: Pentest-Tools.com and Astra Security offer direct online purchase with credit card
- **Best Brazilian options for credit card**: Solyd (12x) and Avant Services (10x sem juros)
- Enterprise-focused platforms like Cobalt.io are more expensive and typically require sales contact
- Brazilian companies generally offer installment plans on credit card, which is advantageous for cash flow

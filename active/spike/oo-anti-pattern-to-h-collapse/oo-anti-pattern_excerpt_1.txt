"This principle is most often associated with Andy Hunt and "Prag" Dave Thomas (The Pragmatic Programmers)."

Additional context from page:
- They described the principle in an IEEE Software column and a post on their website
- IEEE column URL: http://media.pragprog.com/articles/jan_03_enbug.pdf
- Pragmatic Programmers post: http://pragprog.com/articles/tell-dont-ask
- Fowler describes it as "a stepping stone towards co-locating behavior and data"
- Fowler's caveat: does not strictly adhere to the principle; notes concerns about eliminating all query methods

OBSERVATION: The phrases "lines to dewrap" at line 552 is the closest the policy comes to addressing line wrapping.

five layers explicitly forbids the agent from inserting hard line breaks in prose content destined for

DESTINATION (/tmp vs Downloads), but does NOT address LINE WRAPPING WITHIN a plaintext prose output.

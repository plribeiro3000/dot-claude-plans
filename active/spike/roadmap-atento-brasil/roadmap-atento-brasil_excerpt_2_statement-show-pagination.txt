Significance: on the result-declaration side (statement-show), "fully expanded" is defined as
BOTH every panel expanded AND every paginated commissioning list drained to its last page
(paginationDrained). This is the closest existing equivalent to "scrolled to the end" named in
the Atento demand — it is implemented as exhaustive pagination, not a literal scroll-position
listener. The rule-declaration side (plan-statement-show, see excerpt_1) has no pagination at
all (incentives arrive in one GraphQL response), so it only gates on allExpanded() — there is no
scroll/pagination equivalent to drain there, because there is nothing paginated to drain.

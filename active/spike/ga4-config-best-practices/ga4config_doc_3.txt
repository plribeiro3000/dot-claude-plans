TITLE: Google Consent Mode v2 — A Complete Guide (SecurePrivacy)
=============================================================

Four Consent Signals:
1. analytics_storage: "Controls whether Analytics can store data and collect user behavior"
2. ad_storage: "Manages advertising-related data collection including conversion tracking"
3. ad_user_data: "governs the use of personal data for advertising purposes" [NEW in v2]
4. ad_personalization: "specifically controls remarketing and personalized advertising" [NEW in v2]

Key timing requirement:
"Consent signals must reach Google Tag Manager immediately when users make choices"

Most common implementation error:
"tags firing before consent represents the most common and dangerous implementation error"

Default state best practice (for GDPR/LGPD strict environments):
Set all four signals to 'denied' by default before the consent banner loads.
Update to 'granted' on user accept.

CMP Integration:
Consent Mode v2 works through GTM (dataLayer events) or via a Google-certified CMP partner (e.g. Cookiebot, OneTrust, Usercentrics).
For LGPD environments, a CMP must display a consent banner in Portuguese and provide granular accept/reject controls.

Behavioral Modeling (Advanced Consent Mode):
When analytics_storage is denied, GA4 can model conversions using aggregated data from consenting users. Requires at least 1,000 daily events from consenting users to activate modeling.

SOURCE 2: https://cookieinformation.com/lgpd-analytics/
TITLE: LGPD Requirements for Analytics Tracking (Cookie Information)
=============================================================

Legal basis for analytics under LGPD:
Non-essential analytics tracking requires explicit consent under LGPD. Legitimate interests are not a recognized legal basis under LGPD for analytics the way they are under GDPR.

Required elements:
"Consent banners or cookie notifications must allow users to opt-out of non-essential tracking"

Consent must be:
- Free (not bundled with service access)
- Specific (granular per purpose)
- Informed (clear explanation of what will be tracked)
- Unambiguous (affirmative action — pre-ticked boxes are not valid)

Implication for analytics_storage:
Default must be 'denied'. User must take an affirmative action (Accept) before analytics data is collected. Continued browsing or implied consent is not valid under LGPD.

LGPD vs GDPR alignment:
Many requirements mirror GDPR. A CMP that is GDPR-certified and handles Portuguese-language consent flows can typically be adapted for LGPD compliance.

DPA in Brazil: ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados) — has not issued binding guidance specifically on analytics cookies as of 2024, but LGPD Articles 7 and 8 are clear on consent requirements.

NOTE — index awareness (per ActiveRecord Query Discipline): `users` already has an index on
`state_id` alone (`index_users_on_state_id`) and a composite index on
`["company_id", "disabled_at"]`. It does NOT have a composite index covering
`(state_id, disabled_at)` or `(company_id, state_id, disabled_at)`. Any per-country query
shape that filters/joins on `state_id` (or on `states.country_id` via a join) together with
`disabled_at` should have this checked with `.explain` once the exact query shape is chosen —
flagged here as a finding, not resolved.

## Design Pattern: Hub-and-Spoke

Configuration 2 (separate orgs in hub-and-spoke architecture) natively supports duplicate
usernames because different orgs maintain independent identity systems. This design pattern
provides strong isolation while allowing organizations to manage customers, partners, and
employees in separate tenant containers.

## Summary of Okta's Position

Okta's own architecture explicitly supports the same email across multiple orgs (tenants).
Identity is ORG-SCOPED, not globally unique. Apps that use Okta must track which org
authenticated a user. This directly validates the tenant-scoped email pattern as a
legitimate, production-grade identity design.

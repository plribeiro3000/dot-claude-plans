## WorkOS Position — GLOBAL UNIQUE EMAIL

WorkOS is the COUNTER-EXAMPLE: it enforces globally unique email and uses identity linking
to associate one user identity with multiple organization memberships. This is the
opposite of per-tenant scoping — same person, same email, can be a member of N orgs,
but only ONE user record exists.

This is architecturally different from 4Shark's scenario, where the same numeric
employee ID at Mexico company ≠ same person at Brazil company. WorkOS assumes the same
email = the same human. 4Shark's requirement is that the same email = DIFFERENT humans.

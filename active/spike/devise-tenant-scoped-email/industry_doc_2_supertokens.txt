## Interpretation

The SuperTokens architecture explicitly allows the same email to be a valid identity in
MULTIPLE tenants — the composite key is (appId, tenantId, email), not just (email).
This is a production-grade open-source auth system (>12k GitHub stars) that treats
per-tenant email scoping as its primary multi-tenant model.

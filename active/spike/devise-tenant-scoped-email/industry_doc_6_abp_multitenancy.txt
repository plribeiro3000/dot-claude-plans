## ABP Multi-Tenant Design (Isolated vs Shared)

ABP's framework distinguishes two modes:
- ISOLATED (per-tenant): email uniqueness is per-tenant. Default in ABP.
- SHARED: email uniqueness becomes GLOBAL. Requires switching to the shared model
  explicitly.

Post-login, users with multiple tenant memberships see "a tenant selection screen after
credentials are verified." Then a "tenant switcher" allows jumping between contexts
without signing out.

## Significance for 4Shark

ABP's ISOLATED mode (per-tenant email uniqueness) is the DEFAULT — this is the industry
norm for multi-tenant isolation. The SHARED mode is the opt-in exception for when you
need the same human to span multiple orgs.

In 4Shark's case, the same email = DIFFERENT HUMANS (different country, different
company), so the ISOLATED (per-tenant) model is the correct match for the business
requirement.

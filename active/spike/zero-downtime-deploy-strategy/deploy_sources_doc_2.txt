SOURCE F: Sidekiq Wiki — Deployment
URL: github.com/sidekiq/sidekiq/wiki/Deployment
STATUS: VERIFIED (fetched 2026-06-24)
=============================================================

Full verbatim in deploy_sources_doc_1.txt (Source 9).

CRITICAL FOR 4SHARK: Sidekiq's default TERM timeout is 25 seconds. The engineer's
concern about jobs "being processed mid-deploy" is directly addressed by Sidekiq's
design: jobs are re-enqueued if they cannot complete within the timeout. However,
the Iteration pattern (Sidekiq 7.3+) enables true resumability for long-running jobs,
which is the appropriate mechanism for jobs that rename/restructure Redis keys.

CRITICAL FINDING: The article identifies Resque vs Sidekiq as two different philosophies:
- Sidekiq: kills and re-enqueues after timeout (25s default); fast but risks mid-job kills
- Resque: waits indefinitely; safe but risks infinite loops

4Shark uses Sidekiq. The engineer's concern about "groupfication documento" jobs being
mid-deploy is addressed by: (a) Sidekiq re-enqueue on TERM, (b) Iteration pattern for
resumability, OR (c) maintenance window to guarantee no in-flight processing.

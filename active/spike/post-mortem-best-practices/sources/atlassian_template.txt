=== VERBATIM SECTION STRUCTURE ===

SECTION 1 — Incident Summary:
Concise overview covering what occurred, triggering factors, severity level, and duration of impact.

SECTION 2 — Leadup:
Chronological events preceding the incident, including prior changes that introduced latent bugs.

SECTION 3 — Fault:
Explanation of how the implemented change failed to function as designed, with supporting visualizations.

SECTION 4 — Impact:
Effects on both internal and external users, including support ticket volume.

SECTION 5 — Detection:
When and how the team identified the problem, plus opportunities to accelerate discovery.

SECTION 6 — Response:
Who responded, timing, actions taken, and any response obstacles.

SECTION 7 — Recovery:
Methods restoring service and determining resolution completion.

SECTION 8 — Timeline:
Detailed UTC-normalized chronology of leadup events, impacts, escalations, and decisions.

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS SECTION:
The template employs The Five Whys technique: "Begin with a description of the impact and ask why it occurred"
— iteratively questioning until reaching underlying causes rather than symptoms.

SECTION 9 — Root Cause:
The fundamental issue requiring change to prevent recurrence.

SECTION 10 — Backlog Check:
Assessment of prior unaddressed work items.

SECTION 11 — Recurrence:
Identification of related past incidents.

SECTION 12 — Lessons Learned:
Response effectiveness and improvement opportunities.

SECTION 13 — Corrective Actions:
Preventive measures with ownership and completion targets.

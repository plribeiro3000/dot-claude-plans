=== CROSS-SOURCE STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS ===

Sections present in ALL four real post-mortems:
- Summary / what happened (brief)
- Timeline with timestamps
- Root cause / what went wrong
- Impact (quantified)
- Remediation / what changed

Sections present in SOME but not all:
- Explicit "Action Items" table (present: PagerDuty, Cloudflare 2024; absent: GitLab, AWS)
- Architecture/background explanation (present: Cloudflare; absent: GitLab, AWS)
- "What went well" section (absent from all real examples; present in templates)
- Messaging/communications section (present: PagerDuty template; absent from published examples)

Pattern: Real published post-mortems tend to be more narrative and less template-rigid than prescribed templates.

Language: Portuguese (pt-BR)
Tool: Gamma.app — project "V3" (copy of V2)
Status: Partially complete — superseded by V4 planning


Design principles applied
- Audience: non-business franchisees (doctors, hairdressers, etc.) with 2-3 salespeople
- One big bold title + at most 3-4 impact words per slide — presenter explains the rest
- No descriptive text inside cards or bullets
- Images carry emotional tone; text carries concept label
- Dark theme (Onyx), white bold typography

STRATEGIC DECISIONS

What changed from V3:
- Main hook shifted: from "operational chaos" to "tax savings opportunity"
- Pain redefined: franchisees with 2-3 salespeople don't feel spreadsheet/bank pain — their pain is too much work on the owner's plate
- Value prop: less work, not better tools — 4Shark handles the onboarding and setup for them
- Tone: formal and institutional (bank-like trust), not startup agility
- Legal framing: broader than Art. 457 alone — established law, 8 years of practice, Siqueira Castro
- Removed: aggressive "network already chose" framing — replaced with opportunity framing
- Added: calculator demo as live closing argument, Kalaes special conditions

Framing notes:
- Art. 457 paragraph 2 CLT: in place since 2017, widely adopted in private sector, TST jurisprudence consolidated. Not yet government sector (different legal regime), but well established in commerce and industry. Safe to say: "already adopted by thousands of companies, legal framework is mature and validated."
- Siqueira Castro: "one of the largest law firms in Brazil" — do NOT say "largest labor firm" (unconfirmed)
- Banco BV: do NOT mention publicly (partnership not disclosed)
- 4Shark since 2018: 8 years structuring this model


NARRATIVE ARC

Oportunidade > Credibilidade > Simplicidade > Economia > Proximo passo

- Reference: Think Banco Itau, BTG Pactual — premium, trustworthy, established
- Images: Real people in corporate settings, institutional environments, NOT tech dashboards or abstract visuals
- Typography: Same bold white on dark — but less aggressive framing in the copy

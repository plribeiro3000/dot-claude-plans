=== Q5 LGPD PAPERWORK RESEARCH ===

Topic: What LGPD documents does 4Shark need to create or update when adding AI vendors
(Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Deepgram) as sub-processors for the internal AI assistant?

Critical context: 4Shark is OPERADOR of clients' employee PII. Clients are CONTROLADORES.
AI vendors are SUB-OPERADORES. Chain: CLIENT (controlador) → 4SHARK (operador) → AI VENDOR (sub-operador).

This dual role affects which obligations fall on 4Shark directly and which fall primarily on
the client.

SECTION A — LGPD Art. 37 (ROPA / Registro das Operações de Tratamento)
Source: https://lgpd-brasil.info/capitulo_06/artigo_37 — FETCHED THIS SESSION

Verbatim text (confirmed from fetched content):
"O controlador e o operador devem manter registro das operações de tratamento de dados
pessoais que realizarem, especialmente quando baseado no legítimo interesse."

Translation: "The controller and the operator must maintain records of the personal data
processing operations they conduct, particularly those based on legitimate interest."

Title on page: "Artigo 37 - RPA's ou Registro de Operação de Tratamento"

Key point: Art. 37 explicitly binds BOTH the controlador AND the operador. As operador,
4Shark must maintain its own ROPA entry for each new processing activity. Adding an AI
assistant that sends employee PII to an LLM vendor is a new processing activity.

New ROPA entry must cover: purpose (internal support assistant querying operational data),
categories of data subjects (clients' employees), categories of personal data (names,
CPF/identifiers, HR indicators — whatever the internal assistant can query), recipients
(AI vendor sub-operadores: Anthropic; ElevenLabs if voice is used; Deepgram if voice is used),
international transfer basis (Art. 33 Inciso II / contractual clauses under CD/ANPD 19/2024),
and security measures (VPN-gating, Keycloak SSO, PII minimization system prompt).

SECTION B — LGPD Art. 39 (Operador obligations / controller-operator relationship)
Source: https://lgpd-brasil.info/capitulo_06/artigo_39 — FETCHED THIS SESSION

Verbatim text (confirmed from fetched content):
"O operador deverá realizar o tratamento segundo as instruções fornecidas pelo controlador,
que verificará a observância das próprias instruções e das normas sobre a matéria."

Translation: "The operator must conduct data processing according to instructions provided by
the controller, who shall verify compliance with such instructions and applicable regulations."

Chapter: VI — Agentes de Tratamento de Dados Pessoais
Title on page: "Obrigações do Operador em relação ao Controlador"

Key point: Art. 39 creates a verification obligation running from controller to operator.
4Shark's clients (as controladores) must verify that 4Shark (as operador) follows their
instructions and applicable norms. When 4Shark introduces AI sub-operadores, the clients
must be informed so they can verify the new arrangement.

The same Art. 39 principle extends downstream: AI vendors (as sub-operadores) must follow
4Shark's instructions. The Anthropic DPA addresses this via its sub-processor clause.

Note: planalto.gov.br (official source) returned ECONNRESET during prior research.
lgpd-brasil.info is used as secondary source. For binding legal decisions, verify against
the official text at planalto.gov.br.

URL fetched: https://lgpd-brasil.info/capitulo_06/artigo_39
Verbatim quote: Confirmed from fetched content this session.

SECTION C — LGPD Arts. 6 and 9 (Transparency / data subject information rights)
Sources: lgpd-brasil.info/capitulo_02/artigo_06 and lgpd-brasil.info/capitulo_02/artigo_09
— FETCHED (prior session); PARAPHRASES ONLY — verbatim Portuguese statutory text NOT confirmed

NOTE: lgpd-brasil.info returned paraphrases (English-language summaries), NOT verbatim
Portuguese statutory text for Arts. 6 and 9. For binding legal reference, consult
planalto.gov.br (was unavailable — ECONNRESET during research).

Art. 6 paraphrase (from lgpd-brasil.info): Lists ten principles of personal data processing
including transparência (transparency — providing clear, accurate, accessible information about
processing), finalidade (purpose limitation), necessidade (necessity), livre acesso (free
access), qualidade dos dados (data quality), segurança (security), prevenção (prevention),
não discriminação (non-discrimination), and responsabilização e prestação de contas
(accountability and reporting).

Art. 9 paraphrase (from lgpd-brasil.info): Data subjects have the right to receive
information about: purpose, form and duration of processing; the controller or operator
responsible; contact information for the DPO; information about third-party sharing;
and information about international data transfer.

Primary obligation: CONTROLADOR (4Shark's client). The Aviso de Privacidade that data
subjects (employees) receive is the client's obligation, not 4Shark's directly.

What 4Shark must provide to clients: purpose of AI processing, identity of AI vendors,
international transfer destination (US), transfer mechanism (contractual clauses / CPCs
under CD/ANPD 19/2024), so clients can update their own Aviso de Privacidade.

Arts. 6 and 9 verbatim text NOT confirmed from fetched pages — noted here as paraphrase.
No verbatim citation made for these articles.

SECTION D — LGPD Art. 38 (DPIA / RIPD trigger)
Source: https://lgpd-brasil.info/capitulo_06/artigo_38 — fetched prior session (summary context)

Verbatim fragment:
"A autoridade nacional poderá determinar ao controlador que elabore relatório de impacto
à proteção de dados pessoais"

Translation: "The national authority may determine that the controller prepare a personal data
protection impact report."

Key point: The Art. 38 fragment is explicitly directed at the CONTROLADOR — "determinar ao
controlador." The RIPD obligation is the controller's, not the operator's. For the internal
assistant scenario, this would be the client's obligation to prepare the RIPD, not 4Shark's.

ANPD RIPD triggers — UNVERIFIED IN THIS SESSION:
ANPD Resolution No. 2/2022 specifies triggers including large-scale processing, sensitive
personal data, data of vulnerable groups (employees where power imbalance exists), and use of
emerging or innovative technologies (AI assistants qualify). These triggers were not confirmed
from a fetched URL in this research session — the trigger list is based on prior research context
and standard ANPD guidance. For binding reference, consult anpd.gov.br Resolution No. 2/2022
directly.

What 4Shark should prepare to support clients' RIPD: description of AI processing activities,
data categories processed, sub-processor chain (Anthropic / ElevenLabs / Deepgram), security
measures in place (VPN-gating, Keycloak, PII minimization), and international transfer basis.

URL for fragment: https://lgpd-brasil.info/capitulo_06/artigo_38
Verbatim fragment: Confirmed (per prior session research context).

SECTION G — CD/ANPD 19/2024 transfer mechanisms (cross-reference)

For the four transfer mechanisms under CD/ANPD 19/2024, see doc_9.txt Section G and Q4-delta
Dimension 4 in SPIKE.md. No new research conducted here — cross-reference only.

Summary for Q5 use:
1. Adequacy decision (Inciso I) — US: no adequacy. EU: adequacy per Resolution 32/2026.
2. Specific contractual clauses (CPCs) — available under CD/ANPD 19/2024, Annex II.
3. Standard contractual clauses (CPCs-padrão) — ANPD standard form.
4. Global corporate rules (normas corporativas globais) — intra-group transfers only.

EU SCCs (as incorporated in Anthropic DPA, SCCs Module 2) — whether ANPD will accept
EU SCCs as satisfying the LGPD CPC requirement is legally unsettled. See Q4 Section C.

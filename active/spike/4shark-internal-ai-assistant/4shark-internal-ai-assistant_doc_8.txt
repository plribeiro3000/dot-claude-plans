"Amazon Bedrock gives you explicit control over whether your prompts and outputs are

"There is no data retention change to models released before Claude Fable 5. AWS is

Data retention modes table (verbatim):

Mode                | Behavior
--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------
default             | Default means the data retention policy of the model
                    | applies. There is no change to previous model retention
                    | behavior; if ZDR applied previously, then ZDR still applies.
                    | Actual retention depends on the model — consult the model's
                    | terms for specifics. AWS may retain the data for safety and
                    | abuse-prevention purposes. The model provider does not receive it.
provider_data_share | This mode allows Amazon Bedrock to retain and share your
                    | inference data with model providers per their requirements.
                    | It is required for access to certain models.
none                | Zero data retention. No request or response data is written
                    | to durable storage by AWS or shared with the model provider.
inherit             | No opinion at this scope — defer to a broader scope. This
                    | is the default for new accounts and projects.

"Example: Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 require provider data sharing
(allowed_modes: ['provider_data_share']). Customers must explicitly set their data
retention mode to provider_data_share before they can invoke these models."

"A model whose allowed_modes is ['default', 'provider_data_share'] (e.g., Claude
Opus 4.8) — data is retained by AWS only. The model accepts provider_data_share as
a valid mode but does not require data to leave AWS's boundary."

"For models requiring provider_data_share (currently Claude Mythos 5 and Claude
Fable 5): user prompts and completions are shared with Anthropic and retained for
up to 30 days for trust and safety purposes."

"If cross-region inference is enabled for these models, retained inputs and outputs
are stored in destination regions (i.e., the region where your inference request is
processed)."

Article 33 heading: "Casos de permissão de transferência internacional de dados pessoais"

"A transferência internacional de dados pessoais somente é permitida nos seguintes casos:"

Inciso I:   transfer to countries or international bodies providing adequate personal
             data protection (decisão de adequação)
Inciso II:  when controller offers guarantees through:
             a) specific contractual clauses
             b) standard contractual clauses (cláusulas-padrão)
             c) global corporate standards
             d) regularly issued seals/certificates/codes
Inciso III: transfer necessary for international legal cooperation between public
             intelligence/investigation/prosecution bodies
Inciso IV:  transfer necessary to protect life or physical integrity of data subject
             or third party
Inciso V:   when national authority (ANPD) authorizes transfer
Inciso VI:  when transfer results from international cooperation agreement commitment
Inciso VII: transfer necessary for public policy execution / legal public service
             attribution with required publicity
Inciso VIII: when data subject provided specific, prominent consent with prior
             international operation disclosure
Inciso IX:  when necessary for Article 7, incisos II, V, VI hypotheses

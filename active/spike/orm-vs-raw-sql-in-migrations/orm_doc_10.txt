=== Why stub models ===

"First of all, it guards against the case where a model is removed from the codebase but is still being called in a migration. Secondly, it prevents validations from being run, as well as eliminates the associations overhead."

=== Non-reversibility of execute ===

"Examples of a non-reversible may be #execute, #execute_block, or #transaction."

=== Note ===
This source explicitly names TWO benefits of the stub-model approach:
1. Guards against model removal (drift protection)
2. Prevents validations from running (intentional callback/validation bypass)
The second benefit is significant: stub models give you ORM ergonomics WITHOUT running the app's validations/callbacks — which is often exactly what a backfill needs.

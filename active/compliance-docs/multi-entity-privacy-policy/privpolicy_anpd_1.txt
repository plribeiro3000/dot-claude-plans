## First Private Company Fine (July 2023)

Company: Telekall Infoservice (microenterprise, telecommunications sector)
Fine amount: R$ 14,400
Violations:
  1. Treating personal data without legal basis (Art. 7 LGPD)
  2. Absence of a designated Data Protection Officer (DPO)

Relevance to this spike: NO violation relating to CNPJ identification or
controller misidentification in privacy policy. The DPO absence is the only
"transparency" type violation.

## Mass Notification (December 2024)

20 large companies notified by ANPD including:
- Uber
- Serasa
- Vivo (Telefônica)
- TikTok (ByteDance)
- X (Twitter)
- Telegram
- QuintoAndar
- Latam Airlines
- Cacau Show
- Tinder
(and 10 others across technology, telecoms, education, health, retail)

Violations identified (from teletime.com.br fetch):
  1. Missing or unregistered DPO: companies "não indicaram o contato do Encarregado
     pelo tratamento de dados pessoais"
  2. Ineffective communication channels: channels were non-functional or
     non-responsive, preventing data subjects from exercising their rights

Outcome: All 20 companies implemented adequacy measures following ANPD notification.

CONFIRMED: "The article contains no reference to violations involving incorrect CNPJ
registration or controller misidentification in privacy policies."

## ANPD Priority Themes Map (2026-2027)

Four enforcement axes planned:
  1. Data subjects' rights (emphasis on biometric, health, financial data)
  2. Protection of children and adolescents online
  3. Public authorities
  4. Artificial intelligence

40 enforcement actions planned in the data subjects' rights axis over two years.

Note: "controller identification" and "privacy policy CNPJ format" are NOT listed
as priority axes. Privacy policy formatting is not in the 2026-2027 enforcement plan.

SEARCH RESULT NOTE — controller identification enforcement
=======================================================================

Multiple searches for "ANPD" + "identificação do controlador" + "política de
privacidade" + "CNPJ" found no enforcement action, normative resolution, guidance
document, or published decision specifically targeting:
- Incorrect CNPJ in a privacy policy
- Wrong or incomplete controller identification in a published policy
- Multi-entity groups failing to list all constituent CNPJs

Absence confirmed after searching: Migalhas, Conjur, ConvergênciaDigital,
bcompliance.com.br, and ANPD's own publications section.

This absence is meaningful. ANPD's enforcement focus in 2023-2026 has been on:
  1. Processing without legal basis
  2. DPO designation and publicity
  3. Communication channels for data subject rights
  4. Data breaches (Art. 48) and security (Art. 46)
  5. Children's data protection

Grupo Tiradentes Privacy Policy — Section 2 (Abrangência / Scope):

VERBATIM QUOTE (confirmed in fetch):
"A presente Política de Privacidade é aplicável a todas as operações desenvolvidas
no âmbito do Grupo Tiradentes"

Additional notes confirmed in fetch:
- Policy covers: academic units, administrative divisions, cultural organizations, and
  all structures integrated within Grupo Tiradentes per the current institutional
  organizational chart.
- NO specific CNPJ references appear in the scope section.
- Controller identified as "Grupo Tiradentes" (the group), not individual legal entities.
- Data subjects covered include students, employees, suppliers, service users, etc.
- Policy applies "uniformly across all group operations without carving out subsidiary
  entities from coverage."

MARKET PRACTICE SIGNIFICANCE: Grupo Tiradentes is a regulated educational group with
governance compliance obligations. Its use of a single group-level policy without
per-entity CNPJ breakdown is documented market practice for a Brazilian economic group
operating under LGPD.

Migalhas article on intra-group data sharing under LGPD.
Topic: whether sharing data between group companies requires new consent/legal basis.

VERBATIM QUOTES (confirmed in search results):

"o compartilhamento de dados entre empresas, ainda que do mesmo grupo econômico,
necessita de consentimento específico do titular"

"havendo interesse no compartilhamento de dados pessoais de um usuário para outra(s)
empresa(s) integrante(s) do mesmo grupo econômico, com finalidade substancialmente
distinta daquela para a qual o indivíduo autorizou inicialmente, deve-se realizar
um novo pedido de consentimento expresso"

CRITICAL DISTINCTION: This article addresses the legal basis for DATA SHARING between
group entities (a separate compliance question). It does NOT address whether a group
policy can name multiple entities. A single group policy does not by itself authorize
data sharing between those entities — the legal basis for sharing is governed by Art. 7
and is a distinct issue from policy-format consolidation.

Co-controllership (controladores conjuntos) — Brazilian practitioner definition:

"Quando duas ou mais empresas têm participação conjunta nas decisões sobre o tratamento,
seja por meio de decisões comuns ou decisões convergentes, elas formarão a controladoria
conjunta"

ORIGIN NOTE: "Controladores conjuntos" is NOT defined in LGPD. Brazilian practitioners
import this concept from GDPR Art. 26 ("where two or more controllers jointly determine
the purposes and means of processing"). The LGPD does not provide explicit rules on
joint controllership beyond Art. 42 (joint liability for damages).

CONSEQUENCE FOR 4SHARK: If 4Shark's three entities jointly determine the purposes and
means of processing client employee data, they may be classified as joint controllers
(controladores conjuntos), and a single policy should identify all three. If each entity
independently acts as operador for its own set of clients, each entity is a separate
operador and the consolidation question is moot (the clients are the controladores).

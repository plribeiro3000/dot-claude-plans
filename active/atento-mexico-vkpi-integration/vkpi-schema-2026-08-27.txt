DATA FINDINGS (2026-08-27)
  - Duplicate grain WITH conflicting values: the same (NR_RE, DT_DATA, NR_ID,
    NR_SERVICIO_CODIGO, NM_PROGRAMA) appears twice with DIFFERENT Resultados.
    Example: NR_RE 53433, indicator 44 (% Ausentismo), TELEVIA IN, DT_DATA
    20260801 → one row 0, one row 0.565. Reads as two calculation moments
    (pre-cierre / reproceso) stacked on the same grain with no discriminator.
  - Person identifier: NR_RE is stable per person; NR_ID varies per row (it is
    the indicator); RFC is the fiscal id. NR_RE is the carnet candidate.
  - Roles present as their own rows (NM_CARGO_DESCRIPCION): OPERADOR B/C,
    TELEOPERADOR, TELEVENDEDOR, SUPERVISOR BASICO, COORDINADOR DE SERVICIO DE CC,
    ANALISTA DE WFM, AGENTE / BACK OFFICE DE CALIDAD. Supervisors and
    coordinators appear as ordinary rows, one per person·indicator·period.
  - DT_DATA for the monthly sample (% Ausentismo, August) is 20260801 — the
    first day of the period.
